The studio

Two people, one studio

We're Michal Maršálek and Milana Maršálková, a husband-and-wife team. Arcane Blast started in 2022, and we still make every game ourselves.

Being small keeps us quick. We take an idea to a playable test in a few weeks, see how players take to it, and keep building the ones people enjoy.

We like games that are easy to pick up and hard to put down: short battles, clever decks, big hordes, and a good dose of fantasy.

How we make them

  • Game design

    Core loops, progression and economies made for short, replayable sessions.

  • Unity development

    One codebase shipped to iOS and Android, tuned to run well on a wide range of phones.

  • Art and animation

    Characters, interfaces, effects and store art, all made in the studio.

  • Testing and launch

    Store pages, ad creatives, analytics and A/B tests, plus updates after launch.
